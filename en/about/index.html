About the service

Help PC Ninja provides support and IT solutions for individuals and businesses that need a professional, thorough and solution-focused answer.

The service runs mostly on a not-for-profit basis, alongside a small circle of clients who get personal, tailored support.

To help quickly, include in your request:

  • A clear description of the issue
  • How it affects you
  • How urgent it is

Every request is taken seriously, with persistence in finding a solution and judgment about the scope of work needed.

Profit isn't the goal, but in some cases the service involves a fee that covers the time and resources it needs. Cost shouldn't stop you from getting in touch.
